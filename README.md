## About

I am a full-time DPhil student at the [AI4DH Lab, University of Oxford](https://eng.ox.ac.uk/ai4dh/), supervised by [Prof. Tingting Zhu](https://eng.ox.ac.uk/people/tingting-zhu/). My research focuses on modelling electronic health records as event streams, especially for rare diseases, clinical prediction, and few-shot or zero-shot clinical AI.

Before Oxford, I worked as a Senior Research Assistant at the [Digital Medicine Lab, Duke-NUS Medical School](https://blog.nus.edu.sg/liunan/), supervised by [Prof. Nan Liu](https://www.duke-nus.edu.sg/directory/detail/liu-nan). I received an MRes from Imperial College London in 2023 and a BEng from Sichuan University in 2022.
